Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Data Collection & Storage

WA Chat Pinner is designed with strict privacy principles. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal data. All configuration data, including which chats you have chosen to pin, is stored locally on your device within your browser's local storage.

2. Message Privacy

Our extension operates by interacting solely with the Document Object Model (DOM) of the WhatsApp Web interface to rearrange chat elements. It does not read, decrypt, intercept, or store the contents of your personal messages, media, or contact lists.

3. Third-Party Services

Because the extension operates entirely client-side (on your machine), no data is routed through third-party servers, APIs, or analytics trackers.

4. Permissions Required

The extension requires standard browser permissions to function:

  • Storage: To save your pinned chat preferences locally so they persist when you close and reopen the browser.
  • Host Permissions: specifically restricted to `web.whatsapp.com` to allow the script to execute only when you are using WhatsApp Web.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please contact us via the support section on the Chrome Web Store.
